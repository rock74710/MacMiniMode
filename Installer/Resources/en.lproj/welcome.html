Mac mini Mode

Keep a plugged-in MacBook running with the lid closed. Unplug it and it sleeps like a normal laptop again.

Plugged in   Turns on automatically
Your Mac stays awake, even with the lid closed.

Unplugged   Turns off automatically
Normal sleep returns. A closed lid sleeps right away.

Manual   Switch from the menu bar
Automatic, Always On or Always Off.

Installs a menu bar app and a background service. Needs an admin password.
